## Warm cursor companion

Use case: ui-mockup. Edit the provided Windows desktop mockup. Redesign ONLY Clickey's interface and companion, keeping the email app, its placeholder contents, Windows wallpaper, taskbar and real mouse pointer recognizable. High-fidelity delightful product concept screenshot in landscape, no physical monitor or presentation frame. The previous design is too formal and institutional. Make this a striking creative companion interface with personality while remaining respectful and easy to read for older adults. Replace all shield branding and security-dashboard motifs with a friendly original cursor character, a rounded arrow-pointer shape with two subtle expressive eyes. It is a companion, not the actual system pointer. It may look gently concerned but never angry or frightened. Keep long text in a stationary, spacious panel, not a tiny moving bubble. Typography must be large, dark and highly legible; use a friendly rounded sans serif, generous line spacing, oversized clearly labeled controls. Buttons need ample separation. No visual overload. Do not add new prose, slogans, greetings, scores, names, dates, safety statuses, bank details, subtitles, achievements or testimonials. Exact allowed text for Clickey: "Clickey", "Hey, this is a scam, don't click on it", "Evidence", "[Scam signal]", "[Message excerpt]", "Replay", "Mute", "Cancel", "Review". Preserve existing email labels and placeholders. No other text. Keep the whole UI on screen and never clip buttons. Direction A: warm, tactile cursor companion. Soft butter-yellow and peach, plum-colored text, ivory, hints of sage; a polished contemporary 2.5D design with subtle paper texture and soft clay-like details, not glossy enterprise UI. Remove the rectangular sidebar completely. At the lower right, an expressive butter-yellow rounded cursor character peeks above an ivory speech-card-shaped panel. The character has tiny plum eyes and a small rounded peach cheek detail, like a sophisticated friendly desktop toy, not a childish animal. The large fixed panel has an organically rounded asymmetric silhouette and a short speech-tail pointing toward the companion near the highlighted link. Header "Clickey" is large and casually composed, with a labeled "Mute" pill. Render exact scam warning as the large main text. Beneath, one small pale peach inset holds "Evidence", "[Scam signal]" and "[Message excerpt]"; eliminate gray skeleton bars inside Clickey. A large wide "Replay" button incorporates a bold speaker pictogram on a peach inset. Main action "Cancel" is a chunky plum pill with white text, "Review" is a spacious outlined ivory pill. Buttons feel soft and tactile, with subtle bottom-edge depth. The cursor companion next to the actual pointer is the same original butter-yellow arrow character, small enough not to obscure the link. The link outline uses warm amber. Overall composition warm, charming, handcrafted and memorable, with serious readability. Avoid corporate card grids, giant alarm triangles, glossy shields, cold teal styling and multiple duplicate character faces.
