The intended experience:
- A Windows Electron app, with no browser extension.
- A small click-through icon follows the cursor.
- Squeek lives in the system tray with its sidebar initially hidden.
- The user enables browser monitoring once.
- Suspicious visible content automatically triggers the sidebar and a
  spoken warning, without clicking Check.
- Closing the sidebar leaves monitoring running.
- Pause and Quit stop the relevant work.

Current state:

Required acceptance test:

controlled synthetic scam page in the selected browser. Without using
Manual check, Squeek must read the page, detect the scam, open its sidebar,
and speak the warning. Then change to a benign page and verify that stale
warnings and speech do not carry over.

Run this on Chrome and Edge separately. Use owned synthetic test pages,
not private email, banking sessions, or real payments. No extension,
force-accessibility flag, or browser debug connection may be required by
